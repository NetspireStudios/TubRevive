Reglaze Or Replace Your Bathtub?

Choosing whether to reglaze or replace your bathtub depends on more than appearance alone. The condition of the tub, the extent of surface damage, the presence of structural problems, and the amount of disruption you are willing to tolerate all affect which option makes more sense. Tub Revive helps Edmonton homeowners evaluate whether a bathtub can realistically be restored through reglazing or whether replacement is the more practical long-term solution.
