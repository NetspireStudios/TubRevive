Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about bathtub reglazing, from the process and timing to durability, aftercare, cost, and warranty.

01

Process & Timing

Most tubs are reglazed in a single visit, typically 2–4 hours of hands-on work. We’ll walk you through the exact timeline before we start so there are no surprises.

Plan to keep the tub dry for 24–72 hours while the finish fully cures. We’ll give you the exact window for your job and a few tips to protect the surface during that time.

You don’t need to stay for the entire job, but we do ask that someone is available at the start to walk through the space with our technician and at the end to review the finished work.

In short: we clean and de-gloss the surface, repair any chips or cracks, mask off the surrounding area, apply a bonding primer, then spray on multiple coats of Topkote coating. Read our full step-by-step breakdown here.

04

Cost & Value

Reglazing typically costs a fraction of full tub replacement, and there’s no demolition, plumbing work, or multi-day install. Our standard reglazing service starts at $450, well below the cost of a new tub and installation.

Yes — it’s one of the highest-return, lowest-cost updates you can make before listing. A worn tub is a red flag for buyers; a like-new one isn’t.

Our standard reglazing service includes surface preparation, minor chip and crack repairs, masking, priming, and application of the new finish. Re-caulking around the tub is also included as part of the service. Tile reglazing, drain work, and additional repairs can be added depending on the condition of the bathroom and the scope of the project.

05

Safety & Comfort

Yes. We follow strict ventilation and safety procedures throughout the reglazing process to keep the work area properly contained and ventilated. We recommend keeping children and pets away from the work area during application and while the coating cures. Our technician will let you know when the bathroom is ready for normal use again.

There is an odor during and shortly after application, similar to fresh paint. We ventilate the space during the job, and most customers find the smell fades within a day.

We use Topkote products exclusively across every job, chosen for their quality, consistency, and performance.

06

Aftercare & Warranty

Stick to non-abrasive cleaners and a soft cloth or sponge. Avoid scouring powders, bleach-based cleaners, and abrasive pads, which can dull or damage the finish over time. We’ll send you a full care guide after your job.

Skip anything abrasive (Comet, Ajax, steel wool) or highly acidic. A mild dish soap and soft sponge is all you need for regular upkeep.

Every job comes with a standard 5-year warranty against peeling, cracking, and discoloration under normal use.

Contact us if you notice peeling, cracking, discoloration, or another issue with the finish. We’ll review the problem and determine what caused it. If the failure is covered under our 5-year warranty, we’ll arrange to repair the affected area at no additional cost.
